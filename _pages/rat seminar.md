RAT stands for <span style="color:purple">"Researchers Are Talking"</span>. Participants may give an hour talk about their research (invited seminar style) or present a paper of their choice (as an alternative to running a seminar for a whole semester). This seminars aims to give grad students a space to practice giving research talks and learn about what their peers are doing.
